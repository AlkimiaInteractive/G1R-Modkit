2. WHAT YOU MAY DO

2.1 Create mods with the mod kit: change scripts, texts, levels,
    settings and other game data, and add content you made yourself.

2.2 Share your mods free of charge, on the website or platform of your
    choice, for example Steam Workshop.

2.3 Accept voluntary support, for example through Patreon, Ko-fi or
    donations. Supporters must not get anything extra in relation to the
    mod: every version of your mod, including test versions, must be
    available to everyone free of charge at the same time.

2.4 Show your mods in videos and streams, as described in our Video

3. WHAT YOU MAY NOT DO

3.1 SELL YOUR MOD, OR PUT ANY PART OR VERSION OF IT BEHIND A PAYWALL,
    INCLUDING EARLY ACCESS FOR PAYING SUPPORTERS. Any other commercial
    use of a mod or of the mod kit needs our prior written consent.

3.2 MAKE A MOD THAT WORKS WITHOUT THE GAME. A mod must need an
    installed, legitimately owned copy of the game. Do not distribute
    the game, the mod kit or a standalone version of either.

3.3 DISTRIBUTE ORIGINAL GAME CONTENT ON ITS OWN. Ship only what your
    mod changes or adds. Do not extract assets (models, textures,
    music, voice recordings, artworks) from the game to share them
    separately or to use them in another game or project.

3.4 PRESENT A MOD AS OFFICIAL. Do not use THQ Nordic, studio or game
    logos in a way that suggests the mod is made, endorsed or supported
    by us. Every published mod must carry this notice:

        "This is an unofficial modification. It is not made, endorsed
        or supported by THQ Nordic."

3.5 USE CONTENT YOU HAVE NO RIGHTS TO. Including material from other
    games, films, music or other creators without their permission.

3.6 MAKE OFFENSIVE OR ILLEGAL MODS, including hateful, discriminatory,
    sexually explicit or defamatory content, or mods that break the
    law.

3.7 CHEAT OR CAUSE HARM. No mods that give an unfair advantage in
    online or multiplayer modes, bypass copy protection or anti-cheat,
    spread malware, or collect other players' data.

3.8 REVERSE ENGINEER OR HACK THE GAME. Do not decompile, disassemble or
    otherwise reverse engineer the game or the mod kit, and do not
    change the game's program code, except through the tools and
    interfaces the mod kit provides or as far as the law expressly
    allows.

3.9 USE GAME CONTENT TO TRAIN AI. Do not use the game, the mod kit or
    any game content, including texts, dialogue, voice recordings,
    music, models, textures and artworks, to train, fine-tune or
    otherwise develop an artificial intelligence or machine learning
    model. This also applies to mods made by others.

3.10 IMITATE THE GAME'S ACTORS WITH AI. Do not use AI to imitate the
     voice or likeness of the game's voice actors or other performers,
     for example to create new dialogue in an actor's voice.
