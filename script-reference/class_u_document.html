Detailed Description

Document, readable by the Player.

The structure is as follows:

Documents are made up of Segments. Segments are made up of Sections.

A section is what will actually be written in the UI: A paragraph, a list, headings, images, etc.

The game may add Segments dynamically - ie. when the story progresses - to fill the document with more content.
