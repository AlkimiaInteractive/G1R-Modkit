In this tutorial, you will learn how to upload your own mod to the SteamWorkshop. For this, you need to have a "local mod", one that has not been installed through the workshop, ie. the one created in the "First Mod"-Tutorial.

## Step 1: Find your mod in the Mod Management UI

Start the game into *Mod Management UI* via Steam Launch Options or by passing the `-ModManagementUI` commandline flag. Find the mod you want to upload, select it, and click the **Upload to Steam Workshop** button on the details panel of the mod on the lower right.

## Step 2: Preparing Steam Workshop Upload

If this is the initial upload (and not an update to your mod), you will need to request a SteamWorkshop Item ID first:

 * Click the **Prepare Steam Workshop Item** button right at the top.
 * After a short time, the button should vanish and be replaced with a long number: This is your mods SteamWorkshop Item ID.

:::note
If this is the very first time of you interacting with the SteamWorkshop, you may get a prompt to first agree their to their License. Visit the SteamWorkshop through Steam to do that.
:::

Once you have your SteamWorkshop Item ID, fill out the rest of the details (Description, Tags, Visibility, Image, ...).

## Step 3: Upload to Steam

Click the big **Upload to SteamWorkshop** button at the bottom.

After the upload is complete, you should now see a "Steam Workshop" link in your mod's Details panel.
