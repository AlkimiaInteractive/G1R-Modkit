Adds a spell level to the spell configuration. Once casting a spell it will start from first spell level and if player continues progressing the spell (maintaining the attack button pressed) will change to the next spell level and so on. With different spell levels we can increase the spell power for each level and attach new visuals to it. Refer to Fireball as a case with more than one spell level.
